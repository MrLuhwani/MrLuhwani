### Hello World! 👋

I am Toluwanimi Talabi, a CS major at _the University of Ibadan, Oyo state, Nigeria_.

I build scalable and useful software, the backend of it to be more precise. Never had skills relating to frontend and UI, I prefer the backend where I have to think through a lot of decisions, and make the right tradeoffs. Hopefully, my eye for good visuals would improve as time goes on.
